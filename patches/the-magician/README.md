# The Magician

Part of the Arcana series of patches for the Empress ZOIA. Inspired by the Chase Bliss
Mood MKII.

Mono or stereo in, mono or stereo out. A micro-looper with a granular engine, a delay and
a reverb. Loop anything and it comes back changed — barely, or beyond recognition — while
you keep playing over the top.

One section per row on the front page: the clock menu, the looper, the granular, then the
delay and the reverb sharing one row, one toggle and one send.

| Switch | Short | Long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | Record → stop/play → overdub | — |
| Middle | Playback on/off | Clear the loop |
| Right | FX on/off | Loop FX on/off |

The left switch has no long press, so recording fires with no latency. One lamp beside the
clock menu reports the loop: dark when empty, red recording, sky blue playing, pink
overdubbing.

The live path stays stereo end to end; only the loop is mono. `Loop FX` picks both where
the loop goes and what the looper records, so effects are never stacked twice.

The clock menu offers eight loop speeds, written as intervals into the connection
strengths — two octaves down to one octave up.

Played and documented at [zoiatheca](https://github.com/sheoak/zoiatheca).
