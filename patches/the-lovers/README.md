# The Lovers

Part of the Arcana series of patches for the Empress ZOIA.

Mono or stereo in, mono or stereo out. An analog-style chorus and vibrato modelled on the
Walrus Audio Julianna. `Blend` walks from dry, through chorus, to vibrato.

Two things the original does not have: a **reverb** after the effect, and **`Env Dyn`**, a
dynamic response where playing harder opens the effect depth — or closes it, turned the
other way.

The cells draw a heart. The effect is in the middle, the reverb and the dynamics on the
two branches, the tone across the top, and the lamps down the centre.

| Switch | Short | Long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | Tap tempo | Ramp to `Rate 2`, back on release |
| Middle | Reverb on/off | MIDI clock sync on/off |
| Right | Bypass | — |

Two menus in the bottom corners: `Clock` sets what one tap is worth, `LFO` picks the shape.

With every knob at zero the patch is a true bypass — the dry runs through a delay of its
own so it stays in step with the wet and nothing thins out.

Played and documented at [zoiatheca](https://github.com/sheoak/zoiatheca).
