# Temperance

Part of the Arcana series of patches for the Empress ZOIA.

Stereo in, stereo out. A graphic EQ you set by pressing the grid. Five bands, seven steps
each, one row per band: press a cell and that band goes there. No knob to turn, nothing to
confirm. The fourth step of seven is flat, so cuts sit to its left and boosts to its right,
two decibels apart.

Four presets sit under the left footswitch. Pressing a cell writes it straight into the
preset you are on, so editing and storing are one gesture.

| Switch | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Left | Next preset, wrapping after the fourth |
| Middle | Mute, latched |
| Right | Bypass |

The second page is the output level — the same row of seven steps, with a hard mute at its
left end and the two meters below it.

The bands do not arrive together: they come in thirty milliseconds apart, low to high, so
the pedal rebuilds two filters at a time instead of ten and the change does not crackle.

Played and documented at [zoiatheca](https://github.com/sheoak/zoiatheca).
